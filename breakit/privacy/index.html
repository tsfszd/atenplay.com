Privacy Policy

Break It!
Neon brick breaker.

This policy covers the Break It! app for Android (package com.atenplay.breakit), published by AtenPlay. Break It! plays offline and has no accounts.

Last updated: October 17, 2026

SummaryOn your deviceAdvertisingConsentAd-free purchasesGoogle PlayChildrenYour rightsContact

Summary

  • Break It! does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your progress, stars, missions, Collection and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • You can remove ads with an optional purchase, processed by Google Play.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and the game does not send gameplay data to AtenPlay.

Information stored on your device

Your levels, stars, Daily Challenge streak, Endless Mode best runs, missions, Collection of unlocked skins and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your Android phone or tablet. This data is not sent to AtenPlay. Uninstalling Break It!, or clearing its data in Android Settings → Apps, deletes it.

The app asks for the Internet and network state permissions (needed to load and show ads), the Vibrate permission (for haptic feedback you can switch off) and the Google advertising ID permission (for ads). It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

A banner sits below the play area, and a full-screen ad is shown between levels, never during play. To deliver, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps.

Depending on your consent and region, ads may be personalised. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can choose Consent, Do not consent or Manage options. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.

In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising through the in-game privacy message.

Ad-free purchases

Break It! offers two optional purchases that turn off full-screen ads: a 7-day ad-free pass and a permanent ad-free upgrade. Purchases are processed by Google Play. AtenPlay never sees or stores your payment card details. The game keeps the purchase entitlement on your device so that full-screen ads stay off; banner ads may still appear.

Your purchase is recorded by Google Play. You can review or restore your purchases in the Google Play Store under Payments and subscriptions.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google Play handles in-app updates and may provide AtenPlay with aggregated statistics about installs, crashes and app performance, and only if you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data.

Children

Break It! is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because Break It! does not keep accounts or game progress on our servers, most data can be removed by uninstalling the game. For data held by Google, use your Google Account privacy settings. For anything else, email us.

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above. We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
