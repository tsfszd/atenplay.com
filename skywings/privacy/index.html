Privacy Policy

Sky Wings
Bird Flying Game.

This policy covers the Sky Wings: Bird Flying Game app for Android (package skywings.atenplay.com), published by AtenPlay.

Last updated: October 17, 2026

SummaryOn your deviceAdvertisingConsentGoogle PlayChildrenYour rightsContact

Summary

  • Sky Wings does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number to play.
  • Your game progress and settings (scores, unlocked birds and levels, coins, preferences) are stored only on your device.
  • The free game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • The game does not sell your personal information, and it has no in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device

Game progress and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your Android phone or tablet. This data is not sent to AtenPlay. Uninstalling Sky Wings, or clearing its data in Android Settings → Apps, deletes it.

The game asks for the Internet permission (needed to load and show ads) and the Vibrate permission (for gameplay feedback). It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sky Wings uses Google AdMob to show ads. To deliver, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps.

Depending on your consent and region, ads may be personalised based on your interests. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Sky Wings asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can choose Consent, Do not consent or Manage options. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.

In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising through the in-game privacy message.

Google Play

Sky Wings is distributed through Google Play. Google Play may provide AtenPlay with aggregated statistics about installs, crashes and app performance, and it only does so if you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data.

Children

Sky Wings is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because Sky Wings does not keep accounts or game progress on our servers, most data can be removed by uninstalling the game. For data held by Google, use your Google Account privacy settings. For anything else, email us.

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above. We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
