Account deletion

Delete your account
Backgammon: The Velvet Table.

Use this page if you can no longer open the app, or prefer to delete your account from the web. You can also delete your account from the signed-in account menu in the app.

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase sign-in account, including your email address and password.
  • Your display name, portrait choice and profile stored with that account.
  • Friend-game rooms you hosted. Other players in those rooms are removed from them.
  • Your moves in friend games you joined, your guest seat in those games, and any pending join request you made.

Settings stored on your device are not removed by this page. Uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings to remove them. Deletion cannot be undone.

Delete my account



Forgot your password? You can still delete your account in the app, or email hello@atenplay.com from the email address on your account. Include "Velvet Table account deletion" in the subject.
