Privacy Policy

Backgammon
The Velvet Table.

This policy covers the Backgammon: The Velvet Table app for Android (package com.atenplay.backgammon), published by AtenPlay. It covers the local game and the online friend-game features that use Firebase and peer-to-peer connections.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Who can use the game

The game is intended for people aged 13 and older. It is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided an account or personal information, contact us so we can review the request and remove the associated information where appropriate.

Information we use

  • Account information: email address, Firebase account identifier, and the in-game display name. Firebase Authentication handles the password; the game does not store your password in its own database.
  • Portrait: your selected portrait is stored as a catalog identifier in your Firebase profile, not as a personal photograph.
  • Friend games: Firebase Realtime Database stores private-room membership and invite data, player names and portrait identifiers, board snapshots, game actions, and connection-signaling data needed to join a room and synchronize play.
  • On-device settings: the game stores your display name, portrait choice, and review-prompt state in the app's private storage on your device.

Text and voice chat

In a friend game, the players may exchange text and optional live voice directly between their devices using WebRTC. The game does not store the chat text or voice stream on its Firebase server. Firebase carries the connection offer, answer, and network connection candidates used to establish the peer connection. The connection uses Google's public STUN service; network connection details, including IP addresses, may be visible to the peer or processed by the STUN service. Microphone permission is requested only when you choose to enable voice.

Service providers and sharing

Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database, provided by Google, process account and friend-game information to operate the game. Google Play services process an in-app review request if the game asks Play to show a review prompt. Friend-room participants receive the game information and any text or voice you choose to share with them. We do not sell personal information.

See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services for more about Google's handling of information.

Advertising

Advertising is disabled in the closed-testing release. Debug builds may use Google's demo ad placements for development. If advertising is enabled in a later release, this policy and the Play Store Data safety information will be updated before that release.

Retention and account deletion

Local settings remain on your device until you clear the game's storage or uninstall it. You can permanently delete your account from the signed-in account menu on the game's home screen. The game asks you to confirm your password, then deletes your Firebase account, personal friend-game data, and hosted rooms.

You can also delete your account on the web at atenplay.com/backgammon/delete-account.

To request deletion without access to the game, email hello@atenplay.com from the email address associated with your account. Include "Velvet Table account deletion" in the subject. We may ask you to verify the request before processing it.

Security and changes

We use Firebase and WebRTC's transport protections to help secure information in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We may update this policy when the game's features or data practices change; the updated date above will indicate the latest version.
