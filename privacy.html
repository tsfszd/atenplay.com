Summary

  • Offline games such as Break It! and Sky Wings do not require an account. CarnivalBound uses accounts and online services, described in the CarnivalBound section below.
  • Offline progress is stored on your device. CarnivalBound also stores account, multiplayer and wallet information on our servers.
  • Our free games show ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Local game progress and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your phone or tablet. For offline games such as Break It! and Sky Wings, this progress is not sent to AtenPlay. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android Settings → Apps, deletes local data. CarnivalBound's server account and records require account deletion separately.

Games may use your device's vibration for gameplay feedback. This does not collect any data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our games use Google AdMob to show banner, full-screen and app-open ads. To deliver, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps.

Depending on your consent and region, ads may be personalised based on your interests. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, our games ask for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can choose Consent or Manage options to accept or decline individual purposes, and you can change your choice later from Settings → Privacy in the game. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.

In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising through the in-game privacy message.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. AtenPlay never sees or stores your payment card details. To confirm a purchase is genuine before unlocking it, the game may send the purchase token and product ID to our verification server, which checks it with Google Play. We do not use this information for anything else.

Google Play services

  • App updates. The game uses Google Play's in-app update service to check whether a newer version is available.
  • Crash and performance reports. If you allow your device to share usage and diagnostics data, Google Play may provide us with anonymous crash reports, including the device model and Android version, so we can fix problems.

CarnivalBound accounts and online play

CarnivalBound is an online carnival board game for teens and adults aged 13 and above. We collect your email address, username, password hash, player identifier, display name, chosen avatar, optional biography and optional gender. If Google sign-in is offered and you choose it, we receive your verified email address and Google account identifier.

Our servers store multiplayer participation and results, progression, Golden Carnival Ticket balances, daily gifts and wheel rewards, friends, clan membership, invitations, presence and last active time. We store chat messages, reports, blocks, feedback and support requests. Your public profile and global messages are visible to other players; clan and direct messages are visible to their participants. Global chat is removed after seven days.

We use these records to operate the game, secure accounts, deliver verification and recovery emails, save progress, process ticket fees and rewards, verify purchases, handle support and prevent fraud and abuse. Purchase verification stores product identifiers and Google Play purchase tokens, never payment card details. Optional rewarded ads use attempt identifiers and Google's signed completion records; two verified completed ads earn 50 tickets.

Hosting and email providers process data to deliver the service. Network addresses and technical service logs help operate and protect it. Google Mobile Ads may process device or other identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, device/app information, ad interactions and diagnostics as described above. CarnivalBound applies Google's restricted treatment for users under the age of consent to every advertising session and sets a maximum Teen ad content rating. We do not request precise location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

Account, gameplay and social records remain while your account exists to provide the service. Delete your account through Settings → Delete My Account in the app, or use the CarnivalBound account deletion page, which confirms ownership through an emailed link. Deletion removes your profile and associated wallet, purchase records, messages, friends and clan membership from the active game database. Owned clans pass to another member or are removed if empty. Google retains its own purchase records under its policies. Records required by law or for an existing dispute may be retained. Uninstalling does not delete the server account.

We use encrypted network connections, hashed passwords and access controls. No system guarantees absolute security. Information may be processed outside your country. We do not sell account information; disclosures may be required by law or to address fraud, abuse or threats to users and the service.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Offline progress can be removed by uninstalling the game. For CarnivalBound, you can edit your profile, block or report players, decline optional rewarded ads and delete your account using the process above. For data held by Google, use your Google Account privacy settings. For other requests, email us; never include your password or payment card details.

Data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above. We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
