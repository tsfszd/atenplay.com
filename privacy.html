Summary

  • AtenPlay games do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number to play.
  • Your progress (coins, unlocked birds and levels, scores, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • Our free games show ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Game progress and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your phone or tablet. This data is not sent to AtenPlay. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android Settings → Apps, deletes it.

Games may use your device's vibration for gameplay feedback. This does not collect any data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our games use Google AdMob to show banner, full-screen and app-open ads. To deliver, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps.

Depending on your consent and region, ads may be personalised based on your interests. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, our games ask for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can choose Consent, Do not consent or Manage options. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.

In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising through the in-game privacy message.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. AtenPlay never sees or stores your payment card details. To confirm a purchase is genuine before unlocking it, the game may send the purchase token and product ID to our verification server, which checks it with Google Play. We do not use this information for anything else.

Google Play services

  • App updates. The game uses Google Play's in-app update service to check whether a newer version is available.
  • Crash and performance reports. If you allow your device to share usage and diagnostics data, Google Play may provide us with anonymous crash reports, including the device model and Android version, so we can fix problems.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold game accounts or progress on our servers, most data can be removed by uninstalling the game. For data held by Google, use your Google Account privacy settings. For anything else, email us.

Data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above. We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.

Questions about privacy or requests about your data: email AtenPlay.
