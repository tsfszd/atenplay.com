Privacy Policy

Pawns & Masters
Ludo & Chess.

This policy covers the Pawns & Masters: Ludo & Chess app for Android (package com.atenplay.ludolegends), published by AtenPlay. It covers the local game and the online features that use our servers: accounts, friends, clans, chat, voice notes, rankings and coin purchases.

Summary

  • The local game works without an account. Online play, friends, clans, chat, rankings and purchases need one.
  • An account stores a username, an email address and a password stored only as a one-way hash, or a verified Google account.
  • Other players can see your username, display name, avatar, rankings and, in your clan, your last-seen time.
  • Chat messages, reports and match records are stored on our server. Voice notes are relayed to the other players in your match and are not saved.
  • Coin purchases are processed by Google Play. We verify each purchase with Google before crediting coins.
  • The free game shows ads from Google AdMob.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Local game

The local game runs on your device. Its settings and your ten most recent completed results are saved on the device only. This data is not sent to AtenPlay, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android Settings → Apps deletes it.

Account and sign-in

To create an account you choose a username, give an email address and a password of at least 10 characters. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash, so we cannot read them.

You can instead sign in with Google. Our server receives the Google ID token that identifies your account, checks Google's signature and requires that Google has verified your email address. We do not receive your Google password. We keep your Google account's subject identifier so that the same account signs in to the same player profile.

We use your email address to send account messages: email verification, sign-in approvals, password reset and account deletion links. Each link is stored only as a hash, works once, and expires within 30 minutes.

To stop one Google account being used on several phones at once, the app sends a random phone identifier (a UUID created when the app is first opened) with Google sign-in. We store it against your account and ask you to confirm before the account moves to another phone.

When you sign in, we keep a hashed record of each signed-in device you trust, so that you do not have to confirm it again.

Profile and rankings

Your profile includes your username, a display name (up to 32 characters), an optional short bio (up to 160 characters), an avatar you choose, the date your account was created and the last time you were online.

Your display name, avatar and game statistics appear on the public leaderboard, which ranks players by coins and results over the past week or all time. Players can search for others by username or display name.

Friends and clans

  • Friends: we store friend requests, accepted friendships and the times they were made.
  • Clans: we store clan names, descriptions, crests, the language each clan uses, membership, roles, join requests and invite codes. Public clans can be found by anyone; invite-only clans are joined with an invite code and a request approved by a clan master.
  • What clanmates see: members of your clan can see your username, display name, avatar, your role, the date you joined and your last-seen time.

Chat and reports

You can send messages in three channels: global chat, friend chat and clan chat. Each message is stored with its text, sender, channel and time, up to 500 characters. Global messages are kept for 7 days and then deleted automatically; the chat screen shows the most recent of them. Friend and clan messages are kept until your account or the clan is deleted.

To protect players, chat can be paused for 30 minutes if an account sends too many messages in a short time.

If you report a player, we store the report with the category you choose, optional details of up to 1,000 characters, the reporter, the reported player and the time. Our staff use these reports to review behaviour and to take action, such as removing content or an account. Reported content may be kept while a report is being reviewed.

Voice notes

In a match with other human players you can record a voice note of up to 10 seconds with the microphone button. The microphone records only while you hold the button, and the app asks for the Android microphone permission first. The note is sent in small pieces through our server to the other players in that match. Our server relays the pieces and does not save them. A player can send one voice note every 15 seconds.

You can decline the microphone permission. The rest of the game works without it.

Coins and purchases

Coins are sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes your payment; AtenPlay never sees or stores your payment card details.

After a purchase, the app sends the product ID and Google's purchase token to our server, together with a Google Play Integrity token that helps confirm the app is genuine. Our server checks the purchase with Google Play and then credits your coins. We store the purchase token, product ID, your account and the coins credited, so that a purchase is never credited twice.

Coins can also be earned by watching a rewarded ad, up to a daily limit. We record the coins you earn, so that the limit and your balance stay accurate.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free app shows ads from Google AdMob: app-open ads, a banner, interstitial (full-screen) ads and rewarded ads that you choose to watch. To deliver, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps.

Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Device data and permissions

  • Internet and network state: needed for online play, sign-in and ads.
  • Microphone: only for voice notes, as described above.
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob, as described above.

The game uses Google's ML Kit language tools on your device to identify the language of text and translate it, for example chat messages. ML Kit runs on your device. The translation models are downloaded from Google when needed. The app does not read your contacts, location, camera, photos or fingerprint.

The app also uses the Google Play in-app review prompt and the Google Play in-app update service, which are handled by Google Play.

Who processes your data

We use the following service providers to run the app:

  • AtenPlay operates the game servers and the database that store the account, social and match data described above.
  • Google provides Google Sign-In, Google Play Billing, the Play Integrity service, Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), ML Kit and in-app updates and reviews.
  • An email delivery provider sends the account emails described above.

Our servers also record technical information, such as IP addresses and error details, needed to keep the service secure and working. We do not sell personal information, and we share it only with these providers or where the law requires it.

How long we keep data

  • Account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • Global chat: 7 days.
  • Friend and clan chat: until your account is deleted or the clan is deleted.
  • Reports: while they are reviewed and, where action was taken, for as long as needed to keep records of that action.
  • Email links: hashed, and cleared after they expire or are used.
  • Trusted device records: until you sign out of that device or delete your account.
  • Purchase records: the purchase token and coins credited are kept so that purchases are not credited twice, and may be kept longer where needed for accounting, tax or fraud prevention.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Online features include chat, friends and clans, and voice notes between players, so they are intended for players aged 13 and over. If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete it. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account in two ways:

  • In the app: open your account settings and choose to delete your account. Accounts with a password must enter the password again; Google-only accounts must type DELETE to confirm.
  • On the web: open the account deletion page, enter the email address and password of your account, then open the one-time confirmation link we email to you.

Deleting your account removes your profile, friends, friend requests, clan memberships, chat messages, notifications, reports you made, trusted devices and email links. If you own a clan, ownership passes to its longest-standing member, or the clan is deleted if no one else is in it. Purchase records are handled as described in the retention section. Deleting the app alone does not delete your server account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, use the account deletion options above or email us. For data held by Google, use your Google Account privacy settings.
