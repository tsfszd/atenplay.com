Privacy Policy

Pawns & Masters
Ludo & Chess.

This policy covers the Pawns & Masters: Ludo & Chess app for Android (package com.atenplay.ludolegends), published by AtenPlay. It covers the offline game and the online features that use our server, including accounts, friends, clans, chat, voice notes and purchases.

Summary

  • You can play the local game without an account. Online play, friends, clans, chat and purchases need an account.
  • Your account uses a username, an email address and a password (stored only as a one-way hash), or a verified Google account.
  • Online matches, friends, clan chat and reports are stored on our server to run the service. Voice notes are passed to the other players in your match and are not saved.
  • Gem purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive a purchase token to verify and credit the purchase.
  • The free game shows ads from Google AdMob.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Account and sign-in

To create an account you give us a username, an email address and a password (at least 10 characters). Passwords are stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash; we cannot read them.

You can instead sign in with Google. We receive the Google ID token that identifies your account and check that Google has verified your email address. We do not receive your Google password.

We use your email address to send account messages such as sign-in confirmations and password reset or account deletion links. Each link works once and expires within 30 minutes.

Online play and social features

When you use online features, our server stores and processes:

  • Profile: your username, display name, avatar or crest choice, and player statistics shown to other players. Your email address and password hash are never shown to other players.
  • Friends, groups and clans: friend requests, clan memberships, clan applications and invitations.
  • Chat: messages you send in the global, friend and clan chat channels. Global chat shows only recent messages, and messages older than 30 minutes are deleted by an automatic clean-up. Other chat messages are kept so that you and the other members can read the conversation.
  • Match data: the moves and results of online matches, so that the match can be played and resumed.
  • Reports: if you report a player, we keep the report, the reported player's name and your reason, so that we can review it.
  • Notifications: messages in your in-game inbox.

Our servers also process your IP address and request details as part of any connection, as all web servers do, for security and to keep the service running.

Voice notes

During a human-only online match you can record a voice note of up to 10 seconds with the microphone button. The microphone is used only when you press the button, and the app asks for the Android microphone permission first. The recording is sent in small pieces through our server to the other players in that match. It is not saved on our servers after it has been delivered. Voice notes are limited to one every 15 seconds per player.

You can decline the microphone permission. The rest of the game works without it.

Purchases

Gem packs are sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes your payment; AtenPlay never sees or stores your payment card details. After a purchase, the app sends the product ID and Google's purchase token to our server. The server checks the purchase with Google Play before crediting gems to your account. We keep the purchase record needed to prevent duplicate credits.

Information stored on your device

The local game saves its settings and your ten most recent completed results on your device. This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings → Apps. Signing out removes your account session from the device.

The app asks for the Internet and network state permissions (for online play and ads), the Microphone permission (only for voice notes) and the Google advertising ID permission (for ads). It does not read your contacts, location, camera or fingerprint data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free game uses Google AdMob to show ads. To deliver, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps.

Depending on your consent and region, ads may be personalised. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can choose Consent, Do not consent or Manage options. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.

In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising through the in-game privacy message.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google Play may provide AtenPlay with aggregated statistics about installs, crashes and app performance, and only if you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Online features include chat and voice notes between players, so we do not allow accounts for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and its data in two ways:

  • In the app: open your account settings and choose Delete account. Password accounts must enter the password again; Google-only accounts must type DELETE to confirm.
  • On the web: open the account deletion page, enter the email address and password of your account, then open the one-time confirmation link we email to you.

Deleting your account removes your profile, friends, clan memberships, chat messages, match records and inbox. If you own a clan, ownership passes to its longest-standing member. Some records may be kept for a short time where the law requires it, such as purchase records needed for tax or fraud prevention. Deleting the app alone does not delete your server account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, use the account deletion options above or email us. For data held by Google, use your Google Account privacy settings.

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above. We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
